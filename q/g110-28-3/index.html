110 年學測 克漏字 第28題

The ice sheets, which once spanned 20 square kilometers, are thinning over five times as quickly compared to a few years ago. 28 already shrunk by 85% to a mere 0.5 square kilometers, Papua’s glaciers will completely disappear within a decade, researchers have predicted.

  1. (A)Have
  2. (B)Had
  3. (C)Having
  4. (D)Having been

答案：(C)　Having

題目編號：G110_28_3（已自動幫你填到回報表單裡）🚩 回報這題有問題

解析

完成式分詞構句 Having already shrunk，表示「已經萎縮了 85%」（在主要子句動作之前發生），補充說明前提狀況，帶出主要子句的推論；Have（原形動詞，不能這樣作分詞構句開頭）、Had（過去式，分詞構句應該用 Having）、Having been（被動完成分詞，方向不合，這裡是冰川「自己萎縮」是主動語態）都不合。

深入解析

你可能會這樣想

句首要填一個字接 already shrunk，你可能直覺想成「這是完成式，那就用 Have 或 Had」。但關鍵在位置：句子開頭如果是分詞構句，就不能用有時態的動詞。 Have 和 Had 都是有時態的形式，放句首會讓整句出現兩個主要動詞（後面已經有 Papua's glaciers will disappear）。分詞構句要用 -ing 形式，而完成式的分詞就是 Having ＋ 過去分詞。

怎麼判斷

還原成完整子句最快：Having already shrunk by 85%, Papua's glaciers will completely disappear ＝ Because they have already shrunk by 85%, Papua's glaciers will completely disappear（因為已經萎縮了 85%，巴布亞的冰川將會完全消失）。用 Having 而不是單純的 Shrinking，是因為「萎縮 85%」發生在「將會消失」之前——Having ＋ 過去分詞就是用來標示「這件事更早發生」。

誘答選項

Have 是原形、Had 是過去式，兩者都帶時態，不能當分詞構句的開頭。Having been 是被動的完成分詞，但冰川是自己縮小，不是被誰弄縮的——shrink 在這裡是不及物動詞，沒有動作的施予者，這種用法不會有被動式。判斷主動或被動，問一句「主詞是自己做，還是被別人做」就好。

常考字詞與句型

Having ＋ 過去分詞（分詞構句，表更早發生）、Having finished his homework, he...（做完功課後，他……）、shrink by 85%（萎縮 85%）、shrink from A to B（從 A 縮減到 B）、within a decade（十年之內）、indigenous（原住民的）。

Glaciers are usually associated with colder climates. But some mountain ranges of South America, Africa, and Indonesia are also 26 glaciers and ice sheets. These tropical glaciers serve as a major source of water supply to one-sixth of the world's population.

A newly published study, however, revealed that rising temperatures are posing an 27 threat to the last tropical glaciers in Indonesia's Papua region. The ice sheets, which once spanned 20 square kilometers, are thinning over five times as quickly compared to a few years ago. 28 already shrunk by 85% to a mere 0.5 square kilometers, Papua's glaciers will completely disappear within a decade, researchers have predicted.

The disappearance of the glaciers will also leave a significant 29 impact. For many indigenous Papuans, the ice sheets represent a sacred symbol. "The mountains and valleys are the arms and legs of their god and the glaciers are the head," explained the head researcher. The study warned that Papua's glaciers are only the "first to go" if greenhouse gases are 30 and temperatures continue rising. And this is certainly a serious warning about the depressing fate of other glaciers around the world.

中譯：冰河通常讓人聯想到寒冷的氣候。但南美洲、非洲和印尼的部分山脈，其實也擁有冰河與冰原。這些熱帶冰河是全球六分之一人口的重要水源之一。然而，一項最新發表的研究顯示，氣溫上升正對印尼巴布亞地區僅存的熱帶冰河構成迫切的威脅。這片曾經廣達 20 平方公里的冰原，融化速度比幾年前快了五倍以上。研究人員預測，這片已縮減 85%、僅剩 0.5 平方公里的巴布亞冰河，將在十年內完全消失。冰河的消失也將帶來重大的文化衝擊——對許多巴布亞原住民而言，這片冰原象徵著神聖的意義。「這些山脈與河谷是他們神祇的手腳，冰河則是頭部，」研究負責人如此解釋。這項研究警告，若溫室氣體排放不受控制、氣溫持續上升，巴布亞的冰河恐怕只是「第一個消失」的冰河，這無疑也對全球其他冰河令人擔憂的命運，發出了嚴正的警訊。

  3. Researchers expect the ice will vanish within a decade, a loss striking at sacred meaning for indigenous Papuans, who see the mountains as their god's limbs and the glaciers as the head; the study warns further glaciers will follow if greenhouse gases keep rising.
