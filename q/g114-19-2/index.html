114 年學測 克漏字 第19題

慣用語／片語線索：句內答對率 73%
答對率 73%：當年每 100 位考生，有 73 位答對。

You can take some 19 to help avoid the discomfort.

  1. (A)special opportunities
  2. (B)preventive measures
  3. (C)potential risks
  4. (D)significant advantages

答案：(B)　preventive measures

題目編號：G114_19_2（已自動幫你填到回報表單裡）🚩 回報這題有問題

解析

語意「你可以採取一些『預防措施』來幫助避免不適」，preventive measures 符合，呼應後文列舉具體建議（選前排座位等）；special opportunities（特殊機會）、potential risks（潛在風險）、significant advantages（顯著優勢）都不合。

深入解析

你可能會這樣想

measure 你最熟的是動詞「測量」和名詞「尺寸」，所以 take some measures 容易被讀成「做一些測量」。measure 當可數名詞、而且用複數時，意思是措施、對策，take measures 就是「採取措施」。

怎麼判斷

空格後緊接著 to help avoid the discomfort（來幫助避免不適），已經點明目的是「避免」。再看後面三句：reserve seats where motion is felt the least（訂晃動最小的座位）、Looking out into the distance... can help（望向遠方有幫助）、take medicine before your ride（搭車前先服藥）——列出來的全是事先做、用來預防的動作，所以是 preventive measures（預防措施）。

誘答選項

special opportunities（特殊機會）與 significant advantages（顯著優勢）都是正面的好處，但後面列的是動作不是好處；potential risks（潛在風險）方向相反，你不會「採取風險」來避免不適。

常考字詞與句型

take measures／steps to V（採取措施／步驟來做……）、preventive measures（預防措施）、safety measures（安全措施）、prevent A from Ving（防止 A 做……）、reduce the risk of（降低……的風險）、motion sickness（暈動症）。

If you have ever been sick to your stomach on a rocking boat or a bumpy car ride, you know the discomfort of motion sickness. It can 16 suddenly, progressing from a feeling of uneasiness to a cold sweat. Soon, it can lead to dizziness, nausea, and vomiting.

Motion sickness occurs when the signals your brain receives from your eyes, ears, and body 17 . Your brain doesn't know whether you are stationary or moving when these parts send conflicting information: One part of your balance-sensing system detects that your body is moving, but the other parts 18 . Your brain's confused reaction makes you feel sick. You may experience the discomfort from the motion of cars, boats, or amusement park rides. You may also get sick from playing video games, or looking through a microscope.

You can take some 19 to help avoid the discomfort. If you are traveling, reserve seats where motion is felt the least, such as the front row of a car or forward cars of a train. Looking out into the distance from the vehicle can help 20 . You can also take medicine before your ride to avoid or reduce nausea and vomiting.

中譯：如果你曾在搖晃的船上或顛簸的車程中感到反胃想吐，你一定明白暈動症有多麼不舒服。它可能突然發作，從一開始隱約的不適感，逐漸演變成冒冷汗，接著可能引發頭暈、噁心和嘔吐。暈動症的發生，是因為大腦從眼睛、耳朵和身體所接收到的訊號彼此不一致——當這些身體部位傳來相互矛盾的資訊時，大腦便無法判斷你究竟是靜止不動，還是正在移動：你身體的其中一套平衡感應系統偵測到身體正在移動，但其他部位卻沒有偵測到同樣的訊息。大腦因此感到混亂，這種混亂的反應正是讓你感到不適的原因。你可能會因為搭乘汽車、船隻或遊樂園設施而感到暈眩不適，也可能因為玩電子遊戲，或透過顯微鏡觀察物體而感到不舒服。你可以採取一些預防措施來避免這種不適感——如果你正在旅行，可以預訂晃動感受最小的座位，例如汽車前座或火車前段的車廂。將視線望向車外遠處，也有助於緩解不適。你也可以在搭乘交通工具前先服藥，以預防或減輕噁心和嘔吐的症狀。
📋 段落大意（3 段）
  1. Motion sickness, familiar to anyone who has felt ill on a rocking boat or bumpy car ride, can come on abruptly, moving from unease to cold sweat and then dizziness, nausea and vomiting.
  2. It arises when signals reaching the brain from eyes, ears and body conflict: one part of the balance system senses movement while the rest sense none, and the brain's confusion produces the sickness.
  3. Video games and microscopes can trigger it too; travelers can reduce the discomfort by choosing seats where motion is least, such as the front of a car or the forward cars of a train, by gazing into the distance, and by taking medicine before the journey.
