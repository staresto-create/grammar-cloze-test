There are two kinds of heroes: heroes who shine in the face of great danger, who perform an 21 act in a difficult situation, and heroes who live an ordinary life like us, who do their work 22 by many of us, but who 23 a difference in the lives of others.

  1. (A)make
  2. (B)do
  3. (C)tell
  4. (D)count

答案：(A)　make

題目編號：G95_23_2（已自動幫你填到回報表單裡）🚩 回報這題有問題

解析

【句型】make a difference 固定片語「造成影響/帶來改變」；do／tell／count 都不搭配 difference 這個受詞。

深入解析

你可能會這樣想

這題你多半直接就選對了，因為 make a difference 太常見。但值得停一下看清楚它到底在說什麼：difference 是「差別」，make a difference 字面上是「造出一個差別」——本來會是這樣，因為你做了某件事而變成那樣，中間那段落差就是你造成的。所以中文譯成「帶來改變、產生影響」。

怎麼判斷

直接看受詞 a difference 要配哪一個動詞。這是搭配關係，不是文法關係，四個動詞裡只有 make 配得上。後面的 in the lives of others 指出改變發生在誰身上，也就是 make a difference in 或 make a difference to sth（對……造成改變）。

干擾選項

do a difference 這個說法不存在。tell 要配 difference 時是另一組搭配 tell the difference between A and B（分辨 A 與 B 的差別），受詞前面必須有 the，而且句意是「看出差別」不是「造成差別」。count 配的是 count on（依靠）或 count as（算作），不接 difference 當受詞。再補一個常一起考的：make a difference 是造成改變，make no difference 則是「沒差、無所謂」（It makes no difference to me.），兩者不要混。

常考字詞與句型

make a difference to sth（對……帶來改變）、make no difference（沒有差別、無所謂）、tell the difference between A and B（分辨兩者的差別）、count on sb（依靠某人）、do good（有好處）、a selfless act（無私的舉動）。

There are two kinds of heroes: heroes who shine in the face of great danger, who perform an 21 act in a difficult situation, and heroes who live an ordinary life like us, who do their work 22 by many of us, but who 23 a difference in the lives of others.

Heroes are selfless people who perform extraordinary acts. The mark of heroes is not necessarily the result of their action, but 24 they are willing to do for others and for their chosen cause. 25 they fail, their determination lives on for others to follow. The glory lies not in the achievement but in the sacrifice.

中譯：英雄有兩種：一種是在面對重大危險時挺身而出，在艱困情境中做出令人驚嘆之舉的英雄；另一種則是像我們一樣過著平凡生活的英雄，他們所做的事往往不被大多數人注意，卻切實地為他人的生命帶來改變。英雄是無私的人，他們做出超凡的行為。英雄的印記不一定在於行動的結果，而在於他們願意為他人及自己選擇的理念付出什麼。即使他們失敗了，他們的決心仍會延續下去，激勵他人繼續前行。榮耀不在於成就本身，而在於犧牲。
📋 段落大意（2 段）
  1. Heroes can be categorized into two kinds: those who perform extraordinary acts in danger, and ordinary people who quietly make a difference in others' lives.
  2. True heroism is defined by selflessness, sacrifice, and the willingness to help others, regardless of whether the attempt succeeds or fails.
