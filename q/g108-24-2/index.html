This eco-friendly leather has clear 24 for the environment, compared to real leather and synthetic leathers.

  1. (A)advantages
  2. (B)considerations
  3. (C)opportunities
  4. (D)responsibilities

答案：(A)　advantages

題目編號：G108_24_2（已自動幫你填到回報表單裡）🚩 回報這題有問題

解析

語意「這種環保皮革對環境有明顯的『優勢』」，advantages（優勢）符合，呼應後文說明它更環保的具體原因；considerations（考量）、opportunities（機會）、responsibilities（責任）都不合。

深入解析

你可能會這樣想

advantage 你最熟的用法大概是 take advantage of（利用），所以看到 has clear ___ for the environment 不一定會第一時間想到它。advantage 當一般名詞就是最基本的「優點、好處」，這一格要的正是這個意思。

怎麼判斷

空格後面的 compared to real leather and synthetic leathers（和真皮與合成皮相比）是關鍵：句子裡出現比較，這一格填的就該是「比較之下勝出的地方」。再往下看三句，全部都在為這一格作註解：It requires fewer chemicals（需要較少的化學藥劑）、Its manufacture also leaves a smaller carbon footprint（製造過程的碳足跡也較小）、the leftover material ... can even be used as a natural fertilizer（剩下的材料甚至可以當天然肥料）。連續三個好處，答案只能是「優點」。

誘答選項

considerations（考量、要納入思考的因素）是中性的，不含好壞判斷，接不上後面三個明確的優點。opportunities（機會）指的是可以去做某事的時機，環境不會「有機會」。responsibilities（責任）方向相反，後文講的是好處不是義務。

常考字詞與句型

have advantages for（對……有好處）、have an advantage over（勝過……）、take advantage of（利用；占便宜）、the advantages and disadvantages of（……的優缺點）、compared to／with（和……相比）、eco-friendly（環保的）、biodegradable（可生物分解的）、carbon footprint（碳足跡）、leftover material（剩餘材料）。

The pineapple, a delicious tropical fruit, has been valued for centuries not only for its distinct and unique taste, but also for its miraculous health and medical benefits. But pineapples can also be a fashionable fruit: Pineapple leaves can 21 a substitute for leather.

The idea was developed when a Spanish designer travelling to the Philippines observed a traditional Filipino shirt 22 together with the fibers of pineapple leaves. After five years of research, she created from pineapple leaves a material which, like real leather, can be used for making bags, shoes, and 23 textile products. It is an eco-friendly and biodegradable fiber.

This eco-friendly leather has clear 24 for the environment, compared to real leather and synthetic leathers. It requires fewer chemicals, making it safer for the workers in factories. Its manufacture also leaves a smaller carbon footprint. In addition, the leftover material following the removal of fibers can even be used as a natural fertilizer back in the pineapple fields. 25 its low cost, this innovative material is already being used by many leading fashion companies to make their products.

中譯：鳳梨是一種美味的熱帶水果，數百年來不僅因其獨特的風味受人重視，其驚人的保健與醫療功效也備受推崇。但鳳梨其實還能成為一種時尚的水果：鳳梨葉能成為皮革的替代材料。這個想法的誕生，源於一位西班牙設計師到菲律賓旅行時，觀察到當地傳統襯衫是用鳳梨葉的纖維織成的。經過五年的研究，她成功用鳳梨葉開發出一種材質，能像真皮一樣用來製作包包、鞋子以及其他紡織產品，而且是一種環保、可生物分解的纖維。相較於真皮和人造皮革，這種環保皮革對環境有明顯的好處：它需要的化學品較少，讓工廠員工的工作環境更安全；製造過程留下的碳足跡也較小。此外，抽取纖維後剩餘的材料，還能拿回鳳梨田當作天然肥料使用。加上成本低廉，這項創新材質如今已被許多知名時尚品牌用來製作產品。

  2. A Spanish designer traveling in the Philippines noticed a traditional shirt made from pineapple-leaf fibers, and after five years of research turned the leaves into a biodegradable material for bags, shoes and similar textile goods.
  3. Compared with real and synthetic leather it needs fewer chemicals, so factory workers are safer, its manufacture leaves a smaller carbon footprint, leftover pulp returns to the fields as fertilizer, and its low cost has already drawn leading fashion companies.
